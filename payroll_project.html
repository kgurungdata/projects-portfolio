Payroll Audit & Reconciliation Project

Built to practice the core responsibilities of a payroll audit workflow: reconciling recorded gross pay against hours and rate, flagging possible duplicate payments, and reviewing off-cycle payments such as reversals, overpayment corrections, and duplicate employee merges.

  • Payroll Register — 246 simulated pay records across 4 weekly pay periods and 5 departments, with intentionally seeded errors (duplicate payments, gross pay discrepancies, off-cycle entries) for the audit to catch
  • Audit & Reconciliation — live formulas recalculate gross pay from hours × rate, flag variances, detect duplicate employee/pay-period entries, and assign an audit status to every record
  • Weekly Audit Summary — rollup reporting by pay period and flag type, with a summary chart
  • Findings — a written audit summary explaining what was caught and a recommendation based on the results
Skills: Payroll auditing & reconciliation logic, duplicate-payment detection, Excel formulas (SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, IF, ROUND), conditional formatting, written audit reporting
